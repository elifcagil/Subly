### Description
```
Subly gathers all your subscriptions in one calm place. From Netflix to Spotify, ChatGPT to the gym — always know what you pay, when it renews, and where your money goes.

WHY SUBLY?

Most of us don't know what we really spend on subscriptions. Forgotten trials, memberships that renew silently, surprises on the card statement… Subly removes the guesswork: see your monthly and yearly totals at a glance, and never get caught off guard by a renewal.

FEATURES

• Overview: Your total for this month, the change versus last month, and your yearly cost on one screen.
• Next 7 days: See upcoming renewals ahead of time and cancel unwanted subscriptions before they charge.
• Category breakdown: See whether your money goes to streaming, productivity, or music.
• Quick add: Add popular services from a built-in catalog in seconds, or create your own.
• Calendar view: See which payment lands on which day throughout the month.
• Calendar export: Optionally add renewals to your own calendar and get reminded.
• Widgets: See upcoming renewals and your monthly total right on your home screen.
• Multiple currencies: Track subscriptions in the currency you choose.
• Dark mode: Carefully designed light and dark themes that are easy on the eyes.

PRIVACY FIRST

• No account, no sign-in, no email required.
• Your subscription data stays on your device.
• No ads, no tracking, no data selling.

Take control of your subscriptions today. Download, add your subscriptions, relax.

Questions or suggestions: elifcagil.dev@gmail.com
```

### Support URL için yapılması gereken (bir kere)
`docs/support/index.html` oluşturuldu (gizlilik sayfasıyla aynı tasarım).
Çalışması için GitHub Pages açık olmalı:

1. Değişiklikleri commit'le ve GitHub'a push'la.
2. GitHub → Subly repo → **Settings → Pages** → Source: **Deploy from a branch**,
   Branch: **main**, Folder: **/docs** → Save.
3. Birkaç dakika sonra iki URL de yayında olur:
   - `https://elifcagil.github.io/Subly/support/`
   - `https://elifcagil.github.io/Subly/privacy/`
4. App Store Connect'e girmeden önce ikisini de tarayıcıda açıp doğrula
   (Apple, çalışmayan Support URL yüzünden reddedebilir).
